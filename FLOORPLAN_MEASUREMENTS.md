## Notes for redesign scale-checking
- Ceiling height not explicitly dimensioned on these plans — use standard
  ~2.7–3.0 m Greek residential ceiling height unless a room's sloped/vaulted
  ceiling (e.g. the kitchen window nook, the half bath) indicates otherwise
  from photos.
- Living room (6.20 m × 5.50 m) and dining room (4.71 m wide) are the
  largest rooms — furniture scaled for these should read as generously
  sized, not cramped.
- Master bedroom (rear, first floor) is 4.62 m × 3.20 m — a queen bed
  (~1.6 m × 2.0 m) leaves comfortable clearance on both sides, consistent
  with what's already been used in that room's redesign.
- Two bathrooms + one ground-floor WC total for the unit.
